Overview

3-Axis Inclination Monitoring for Infrastructure

The Wireless Tiltmeter is a compact, 3-axis inclinometer built for long-term, unattended monitoring of slopes, structures, rail tracks and tunnelling works. It reports high-resolution inclination values plus measurement uncertainty to support noise filtering and trustworthy trend analysis.

Available in two variants — XHP with external antenna for maximum range in high-precision applications, and IR with internal antenna for compact, vandal-resistant installations such as rail track monitoring.

Core Capabilities

What Makes the Wireless Tiltmeter Different

Purpose-built for unattended tilt monitoring with edge intelligence and long-range wireless connectivity.

3-Axis Precision

Three-axis MEMS accelerometer with gravity-referenced inclination readings across ±90° range. Factory-calibrated for drift-free, repeatable measurements in any orientation.

Smart Event Detection

Motion detection sensor triggers measurements from variations in absolute angle, angular velocity or acceleration. Configurable delays and cooldown periods for efficient alerting.

Ultra-Long Battery Life

Up to 10 years of unattended operation on 2 x 3.6V C-size batteries. User-replaceable without special tools. Extended modes reach >25 years at 6hr reporting intervals.

Long-Range Wireless

LoRaWAN communications reaching up to 15 km in open terrain. External antenna variant (XHP) for maximum range; internal antenna (IR) for compact, vandal-resistant installations.

IP68 Rugged Design

Industrial-grade IP68 enclosure for full submersion protection. Operating range from −40°C to +80°C — suitable for tunnels, mines, bridges and exposed infrastructure.

Cloud & Edge Management

Configure locally via CMT Edge for single-network deployments, or connect to CMT Cloud for multi-project oversight. Bluetooth and USB-C configuration via the Worldsensing App.

Applications

Got Questions About the Wireless Tiltmeter?

A Tiltmeter measures small changes in an object's inclination — rotation, settlement, track cant or slow tilt of slopes and structures. It reveals gradual trends and sudden shifts that indicate instability or misalignment.

Precision MEMS accelerometers sense the gravity vector and temperature. Onboard firmware converts those signals into angle values with compensation and filtering, timestamps each sample, and forwards readings to your gateway or cloud for analysis.

Tiltmeters capture very small, repeatable changes — much smaller than what the human eye detects. With ±0.0001° resolution and repeatability as low as <0.0003°, they are suitable for the most demanding engineering applications.

The device runs in low-power modes and can operate unattended for up to 10 years at typical reporting intervals. Batteries are user-replaceable without tools. Routine checks of physical mounts and antenna are recommended during scheduled site visits.

Yes — data is transmitted via LoRa/LoRaWAN gateways to cloud endpoints in common data formats and APIs. It integrates with dashboards, alarm systems, asset-management software, and custom webhooks can be configured.
