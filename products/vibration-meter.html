Overview

Automated Vibration Monitoring for Infrastructure Safety

The Wireless Vibration Meter is a tri-axial sensor built for long-term, continuous vibration monitoring of bridges, tunnels, buildings, and industrial assets. It leverages an exception-based edge algorithm that automatically detects and reports threshold breaches based on PPV and frequency parameters.

Available with internal antenna (Vibration-IR) for compact, streamlined deployment in general assessment applications.

Operational Modes

Two Powerful Analysis Modes

Configurable operational modes with advanced algorithms for various vibration standards and assessment requirements. Available with internal antenna for compact deployment.

PPV-Based Mode

Reports Peak Particle Velocity (PPVx,y,z in mm/s), frequency (fx,y,z in Hz), temperature (ºC), and Peak Vector Sum (PVS). Velocity range: 0-60 mm/s (±0.01 mm/s). Frequency output range: 1-100 Hz (±0.5 Hz). Ideal for blast vibration monitoring and structural response assessment.

MTVV-Based Mode

Reports maximum transient vibration value (LAW-x,y,z in dBm) and temperature (ºC). Acceleration range: ±2g. Frequency weighting: Wm. Designed for human comfort assessment and continuous vibration monitoring per ISO standards.

Alert mode with secondary threshold enables automatic reporting of critical events via real-time notifications.

Industrial-grade IP68 enclosure for full submersion protection. Operating range from −40°C to +80°C — suitable for the harshest field conditions.

Edge Algorithm

Exception-based detection automatically identifies threshold breaches based on PPV/LAW and frequency parameters, eliminating manual data retrieval and reducing false alarms.

Cloud & Edge Management

Mobile App Configuration
