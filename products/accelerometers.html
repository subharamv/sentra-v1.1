Measure the Movements That Matter

Core Capabilities

What Makes Our Accelerometer Different

Purpose-built for precision motion monitoring with edge intelligence and robust field-ready design.

3-Axis MEMS Technology

High-resolution triaxial capacitive MEMS sensing across X, Y, and Z axes. Converts even the smallest accelerations into stable electrical signals with internal filtering and temperature compensation.

Dual Range Options

Available in ±2g and ±6g dynamic range options to suit different applications — from subtle structural deflection to more dynamic vibration and machinery monitoring.

Rugged IP65 Design

Sealed IP65 housing with molded nylon construction and brass mounting inserts. Operating temperature range of −40°C to +80°C for harsh outdoor and industrial environments.

Long-Range Connectivity

Integrates with GeoNet LoRaWAN, Cellular, Wi-Fi, and Satellite data loggers for flexible long-range data transmission directly to your cloud dashboard.

PPV & MTVV Modes

Dual operation modes — PPV-based for peak particle velocity and MTVV-based for whole-body vibration assessment. Covering 0.5 – 250 Hz frequency range.

Integrated Self-Test

Built-in self-test function and low-pass filter ensure reliable operation and clean data transmission. Shielded six-conductor cable for noise-free signal output.
