Why XGRIDS

Reality Capture, Reimagined

Every XGRIDS device is built around multi-sensor SLAM fusion and on-device processing — no external tracking, no dedicated survey crew required.

Multi-SLAM + RTK Fusion

LiDAR, visual and inertial SLAM fused with onboard RTK for centimetre-level absolute accuracy without ground control targets.

Native 3D Gaussian Splatting

Point clouds and photoreal Gaussian Splat models generated together — deliver both survey data and immersive walkthroughs from one scan.

Walk-and-Scan Workflow

No tripods, targets or stop-and-go setups. Walk the site once and capture complete 360° coverage in a single continuous pass.

LixelStudio & LixelGo

One-click on-device processing via LixelGo, with full point-cloud registration, classification and export via LixelStudio desktop software, plus 3D Gaussian Splatting via LCC.

Open Data Formats

Export to LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY and Gaussian Splat formats — compatible with Revit, AutoCAD, Unreal Engine, Unity and Blender.

India-Based Support

As official reseller, Sentra provides local demos, training, calibration support and warranty service across India.
