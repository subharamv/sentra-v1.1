Overview

Rapid Capture, Zero Setup

The LixelKity K1 integrates vision, LiDAR, inertial navigation and high-performance computing into a device weighing under 1kg. Dual 48-megapixel panoramic cameras and 360° LiDAR combine to deliver relative accuracy of 1.2cm and absolute accuracy of 3cm — without a single tripod setup.

Colourised .las point clouds write directly to a replaceable 256GB TF card, with fast USB 3.1 Gen2 offload to a workstation. A full battery charge takes about 2 hours, and the IP54-rated body operates reliably from -20°C to +50°C, in the field or indoors.
